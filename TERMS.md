## 6. Privacy

PaperLens has no accounts and doesn't collect your data. Your settings, keys and generated explanations are stored in your own browser or app.

To work, PaperLens sends requests to the services you use: your AI provider (the paper's text and your questions), arXiv and OpenAlex (paper searches and downloads), and GitHub (code lookups). Those services handle that data under their own privacy policies.

If you use a website run by someone else, that operator's server handles your requests, including your API key while a request is in progress.
